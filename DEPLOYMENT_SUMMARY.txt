SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: "App is not installable"
A: Verify HTTPS enabled, hard refresh, check manifest in DevTools

Q: "Service Worker not updating"
A: Hard refresh, unregister SW, increment VERSION in service-worker.js

Q: "Offline mode not working"
A: Check DevTools → Cache Storage, verify SW is active, hard refresh

Q: "Form not submitting"
A: Check DevTools console for errors, verify form has values

Q: "Push notifications not working"
A: Check browser permissions, test on Android, note iOS not supported

See PWA_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md for detailed troubleshooting.
